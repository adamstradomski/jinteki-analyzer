Cobra Bot

A Discord bot that shows pairings, standings and players of Cobra Netrunner tournaments, right in your channel.

Add to Discord

Commands

<tournament> is a Cobra tournament number or a link to any of its pages, e.g. https://tournaments.nullsignal.games/tournaments/5018.

/cobra standings <tournament>
Standings after the last completed round: rank, player, points, Strength of Schedule and both IDs. Before round 1 it lists the registered players.
/cobra pairings <tournament> [round]
Pairings of the latest Swiss round, or of the round you give: tables, players, sides, IDs and results reported so far. Works for single-sided and double-sided Swiss.
/cobra player <tournament> <names>
A card for each matching player: rank, points, SoS, IDs and their current table. List up to 10 names separated by commas to follow a group; letter case and accents don't matter.

Replies are posted in the channel for everyone to see. Top cut rounds aren't supported yet. If Cobra is down, the bot shows the last data it fetched and says when.

Example

Install

Open the invite link and choose:

  • Add to server: the commands work for everyone in that server. Needs the Manage Server permission there.
  • Add to my apps: the commands work for you in any server, DM or group DM.

The bot needs no permissions: it only answers its own commands.

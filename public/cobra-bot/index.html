Cobra Bot

A Discord bot that shows pairings, standings, the top cut and players of Cobra Netrunner tournaments, right in your channel.

Add to Discord

Commands

<tournament> is a Cobra tournament number (4909), its shortcode (QNSF) or a link to any of its pages, e.g. https://tournaments.nullsignal.games/tournaments/4909/players/standings.

/cobra standings <tournament>
Swiss standings: rank, player, Corp and Runner ID, points and Strength of Schedule, and after which round they apply. Once Swiss is over it also says whether there is a top cut and how far it has got.
/cobra pairings <tournament> [round]
Pairings of the latest round, Swiss or top cut, or of the round you give: whether it is complete or in progress, tables, players, sides, IDs and results. A top cut round shows game numbers and who won.
/cobra top-cut <tournament>
The top cut ranking: place, player, IDs, games won and lost in the cut, and seed.
/cobra bracket <tournament>
The top cut bracket as one picture, laid out like the bracket page on Cobra.
/cobra player <tournament> <names>
Players whose name contains the text, with their standing and their game in the latest round. List up to 10 names separated by commas (Alice, Bob) to follow a group; letter case and accents don't matter.

Replies are posted in the channel for everyone to see. Tables are pictures, so they look the same on desktop and phones; long lists are split into up to 5 pictures, with a link to the full page on Cobra after that. Data is at most a minute old. If Cobra is down or the tournament has been made private, the bot shows the last data it fetched and says when.

Example

Install

Open the invite link and choose:

  • Add to server: the commands work for everyone in that server. Needs the Manage Server permission there.
  • Add to my apps: the commands work for you in any server, DM or group DM.

The bot needs no permissions: it only answers its own commands.

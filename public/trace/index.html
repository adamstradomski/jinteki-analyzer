The charts above plot each player against their own turn counter — but Corp's turn 3 and Runner's turn 3 aren't the same moment, and either player can gain credits or draw cards during the other's turn. These same five stats are re-plotted here against the order turns actually started in the log (labels like C1/R1/C2/R2 — Corp turn N or Runner turn N, in play order), so the two players' lines line up in real time instead of by turn number. Compare both and keep whichever reads more clearly for a given log.

How this works

+

This reads it for turn count, when each card entered and left play, each card's net credit contribution, and agenda points scored over time.

  • Corp assets and ice are installed face-down, so the log doesn't reveal a name until they're rezzed. "In play from" for those cards is the turn they were rezzed, not the (unknown) turn they were actually installed.
  • Credits are attributed to a card via its own "uses X to gain N" / "reveal ... and gain N" triggers, plus any "pays N from X" spend that draws from a card's hosted credits.
  • Cost is the credits spent to install, rez, play, or activate a card — net credits is gained minus cost.
  • In the operations & events table, "triggered" counts each play separately (paying to play starts a new one, even on the same turn) — the expanded rows list every play.
  • Identity abilities (cards with a "Faction: Title" name) are tracked as in play from turn 1 for the whole game.
  • Click-for-credit is grouped separately per side rather than attributed to any card. When a card's "gain" line has no number (the credit icon was stripped from the log text), it's read as a click gain rather than a credit — 1 click by default, or a hardcoded amount for specific cards known to grant more (e.g. Nanomanagement grants 2).
  • A card that shuffles itself away (e.g. Spin Doctor's "shuffle" ability) is treated as leaving play at that point, same as being trashed.
  • Start/end-of-game chat badges (GLHF / GG / TY4TG) are detected from simple keyword matches on chat lines — a message that doesn't use these words won't be picked up.
  • Scored or stolen agendas aren't tracked as economy permanents, only in the agenda points chart.
  • Both players are assumed to start with 5 credits and 5 cards in hand, per standard rules.
  • Concession is detected from a "<player> concedes" line — this phrasing hasn't been confirmed against a real jinteki.net concession log, so treat the CONCEDE badge as best-effort.
  • When a card's effect silently adds another agenda to a score area (e.g. Regenesis revealing an agenda from Archives), the points for that agenda aren't stated in the log. They're filled in from a small hardcoded table sourced from NetrunnerDB; an agenda not in that table is flagged instead of guessed.
